Credo &
TypeScript library for credential issuance, reception, and verification. &
Supports configurable DID methods; ledger use depends on the selected method. &
Version 0.7.0 includes support for DIDComm, OpenID for Verifiable Credentials, AnonCreds, and SD-JWT VC. Compatibility depends on protocol versions and implementation configurations~\cite{platform-credo}. \\

\addlinespace[5pt]

Veramo &
Modular library for decentralized identifiers and verifiable credentials. &
Supports \texttt{did:ethr}, \texttt{did:web}, and \texttt{did:key} through plugins; blockchain use depends on the method. &
Supports Node.js, browsers, and React Native~\cite{platform-veramo}. Privacy and interoperability depend on the selected plugins and credential formats~\cite{platform-veramo}. \\

\addlinespace[5pt]

Sovrin Network &
Identity registry originally operated by multiple stewards. &
Uses Hyperledger Indy for identity records, not general-purpose smart contracts. &
Sovrin MainNet is maintained as a read-only archive by a single caretaker steward. Existing records remain accessible, but new ledger transactions are not supported in this configuration~\cite{platform-sovrin,platform-indy-node}. \\

\addlinespace[5pt]

AnonCreds &
Anonymous-credential specification supporting selective disclosure, predicates, and non-revocation proofs. &
Uses registries for issuer verification information and, when supported, revocation status; no specific blockchain is required. &
Defines cryptographic operations and credential presentation mechanisms. Privacy and revocation properties depend on the selected registry, wallet, and verifier implementations~\cite{platform-anoncreds}. \\

\addlinespace[5pt]

EUDI reference wallet &
Android reference implementation for credential issuance, storage, and presentation. &
Uses credential exchange protocols and certificate-based trust without requiring a blockchain. &
Supports OpenID4VCI, OpenID4VP, and proximity presentation. The reference implementation includes demonstration services and does not establish production readiness or wallet certification~\cite{platform-eudi-wallet}. \\

\addlinespace[5pt]

Identity.com &
Specification and reference implementation of the \texttt{did:sol} method. &
Uses Solana programs to manage public DID documents. &
Provides public reference code~\cite{platform-sol-did}. Persistent identifiers can enable correlation, and the DID method alone does not provide selective disclosure~\cite{platform-sol-did}. \\
